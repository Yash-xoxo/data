Recent political developments, marked by strong divisions and increased engagement, have significantly impacted how people participate in elections. We’ve seen a rise in voter turnout across the political spectrum, driven by passionate reactions to key figures and policies. Simultaneously, the rise of social media has fundamentally changed the way political campaigns are run and how politicians connect with voters, bypassing traditional media outlets.  Specific issues, such as gun control and immigration, have become particularly prominent in public debate due to the heightened political climate and the concerns they represent to different groups.  Essentially, the current political landscape is fueling both greater participation and a shift in the issues dominating public discussion.
