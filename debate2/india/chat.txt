[Curious]
What are the most significant achievements of Indian culture in modern times?

[Explainer]
India has been a significant contributor to global culture throughout history, but the last century saw several key developments that have had a profound impact on the world. Here are some of the most notable achievements:

1. Cinema: India's film industry, known as Bollywood, is one of the largest and most prolific in the world. It produces thousands of films each year, many of which are hugely popular around the globe. Bollywood movies often feature elaborate song-and-dance sequences and colorful costumes, and have inspired countless imitations in other countries.
