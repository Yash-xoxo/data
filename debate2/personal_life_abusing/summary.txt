Abuse of personal life can be attributed to several factors such as stress, addiction, lack of self-esteem, poor social skills, or traumatic experiences. To overcome stress and improve self-esteem, maintaining a healthy lifestyle is essential, including adequate sleep, exercise, balanced diet, and relaxation techniques like deep breathing or meditation. It's also important to engage in regular social interaction with supportive individuals. If someone struggles with addiction, it may be caused by genetics, environment, or social factors such as stress, trauma, or family history. Addiction affects the brain and body, leading to changes in neurotransmitter levels and disrupting normal communication within the brain. Understanding how drugs affect the brain is crucial for understanding addiction.
