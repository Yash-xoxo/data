Artificial Intelligence (AI) has both advantages and disadvantages when it comes to preventing illegal activities such as fraud and identity theft. While AI can analyze large amounts of data quickly and accurately, leading to the identification of potential fraudulent behavior in cases like transactional analysis, there have been instances where these methods have failed due to insufficient data or inadequate training.

The effectiveness of AI methods depends on various factors including the type of AI being used, the specific use case, and the level of human intervention required. While humans may need to review results generated by AI algorithms before taking action in some cases, this can be necessary to avoid false positives or negatives resulting from limited data or inadequate training.

Overall, while AI has potential benefits for combating illegal activities through predictive policing, facial recognition technology, monitoring social media platforms and analyzing the impact of new laws and regulations on criminal behavior, it is important to consider its limitations as well.
